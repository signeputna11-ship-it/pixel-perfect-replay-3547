# Workshop test checklist
Test on a phone-sized screen and on a computer.

1. Home → "Discover My Pattern" → answer 10 questions → result page appears (no email asked).
2. Result → "Start your first lesson free" opens Day 1 of the top recommended course.
3. Result → each recommended card opens its course page.
4. Menu (phone): Free audit, Programmes, Member preview, Coaching all open.
5. Each of the 4 course pages: Get Access shows "Checkout isn't open yet"; each curriculum day opens/closes; FAQ items open/close.
6. Day 1 lesson: type in reflections; exercise Next / Back / Skip step / Skip exercise / Fill with example / Edit answers.
7. Copy summary and Download .txt both work.
8. Mark lesson complete → course page shows 1/N progress and "Resume Day 1"; refresh keeps the tick. Tap again to undo.
9. Leave and return to a lesson → reflections are empty (not saved, by design).
10. Pause this lesson shows the grounding message.
11. Keyboard only: Tab through a lesson and course page; every button is reachable and works with Enter/Space.
12. Footer → Privacy & safety opens.
